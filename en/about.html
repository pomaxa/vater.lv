Our story

Experience and Quality

SIA VATER is a Latvian metal processing company that has been providing the highest quality metal processing services in Latvia and the Baltic region for over two decades.

Our company specializes in a wide range of metal processing services - from precision laser cutting to complex metal constructions and kitchen equipment for the food industry.

We pride ourselves on our ability to combine traditional metalworking skills with the latest technologies, providing customers with both individual parts and large-scale serial production.
